1. About Decision Roulette

Decision Roulette is a utility application that allows users to create roulette wheels, enter multiple choices, and randomly select an option by spinning the wheel.

The App does not require registration or a user account.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Decision Roulette may store information locally on your device in order to provide its functionality.

This may include:

  • roulette names;
  • option names and text;
  • option colors and order;
  • images you choose to associate with options;
  • sound and animation preferences;
  • spin settings;
  • language settings;
  • other application preferences.

This information is stored locally in the App's private storage and is not uploaded to an UpApplications server.

If you export or share a roulette, a file may be created only when you explicitly request that action.

3. Personal Information

Decision Roulette does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, or other personal information in order to use the App.

The App does not require a user account.

However, third-party advertising services used by the App may automatically process certain technical information as described below.
